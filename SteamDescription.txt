Small fixes and optional tweaks for vanilla RimWorld, collected in one mod.
Full description of every part: [url=https://github.com/ifchen0/VanillaTuneUp#readme]README on GitHub[/url] (also shown in the mod settings).

[h2]How it works[/h2][list]
[*]Turn each part on or off in [i]Options > Mod settings > Vanilla Tune-Up[/i] (restart to apply).
[*]Fixes are on by default. Tweaks marked (on) only change the interface or wait until you use them; other tweaks are off until you turn them on.
[*]If one part fails to load (for example after a game update), only that part is turned off.
[/list]

[h2]Fixes[/h2][list]
[*][b]Lag:[/b] History tab in long-running colonies; an open right-click menu on the map; trade windows with many items (Auto Seller included).
[*][b]Errors:[/b] [i]Faction leader for X is null[/i]; [i]Exception while ticking lord with job RimWorld.LordJob_Siege[/i] (modded mechs in sieges); quest text shown as [i]ERR:[/i] and untranslatable text in the official translation (currently Traditional Chinese).
[*][b]Prisoners and slaves:[/b] wardens respect food policy during mental breaks, tend bleeders before feeding and feed a whole cell in one trip; wounded prisoners get up for a deadly drug need; banished slaves free their bed (Ideology); farskip home unloads prisoners (Royalty).
[*][b]Animals:[/b] hungry pets find food across the map; cold animals head indoors before frostbite kills babies.
[*][b]Other:[/b] ejected genepacks stay out of the gene bank (Biotech); a vomiting psychic ritual invoker keeps the offering (Anomaly); the "prisoner willing to join" quest can bring back former colonists of any race or xenotype; combat logs keep fights with animals and mechs; the bottom-right search and codex buttons stay aligned.
[/list]

[h2]Tweaks[/h2][list]
[*][b]Interface:[/b] pin resources (on); short pawn names on the map (on); faction icons in caravan lists (on); background music button (on); hide slaves from the colonist bar (Ideology); compact colonist bar; hide and reorder bottom-right buttons; right-drag to reorder the bottom bar, architect and command buttons; tell faction colors apart; mod update notes in the main menu (on, Steam); confirm before closing the game window (on); taskbar letter badge (on, Windows).
[*][b]Letters:[/b] right-click dismisses bundled letters (on); click a letter to jump to its target.
[*][b]Camera and keys:[/b] [b],[/b] / [b].[/b] cycle prisoners, visitors, animals and mechs; the camera follows the pawn it jumped to (on) and avoids the open inspect tab (on); F5 quicksave, Save and quit, Continue; screensaver when idle (on); caravan encounters stay on the current map (on).
[*][b]Colony work:[/b] xenogerm queue (Biotech); nutrient paste "until you have X" (on); smelt and destroy bills keep X of each (on); biosculpter pod lists only colonists who need the cycle, with heal count, age or mood (Ideology, on); field tending and cheaper medicine for minor wounds; armed guards for dangerous hunts; readers face the way their seat faces (on).
[*][b]Prisoners and slaves:[/b] slaves use prisoner beds (Ideology); prisoners help out by personality; calmer prison breaks and slave rebellions; wardens capture downed enemies; default food and apparel policies for new prisoners and slaves.
[*][b]Visitors, caravans and outsiders:[/b] visitors take shelter from bad weather or danger; visitors leave colony food alone; refugees follow an apparel policy (Royalty); caravans abandon all dead at once (on) and take rescued guests along; friends come for wounded guests; doctors rescue friendly outsiders; former colonists and outsiders come back with visitors, raids and settlements; short-stay former colonists and former leaders are cleaned out of the save.
[*][b]Pawn life:[/b] bystanders break up social fights; Shooting or Melee passion pawns practice on trees; Social passion pawns chat over the comms console; psychic rituals sorted by psychic sensitivity (Anomaly, on); hybrid children keep a matching xenotype (Biotech); ghouls can wear apparel (Anomaly); mute labor and birth sounds (Biotech); remove fog of war.
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2][list]
[*]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save. Removing it only drops xenogerm queues; with Break up social fights on, the first load logs one harmless error per leftover fight memory.
[*]Load after Harmony. English and Traditional Chinese included.
[/list]
