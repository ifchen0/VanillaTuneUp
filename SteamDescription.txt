Small fixes and optional tweaks for vanilla RimWorld, collected in one mod.
Full description of every part: [url=https://github.com/ifchen0/VanillaTuneUp#readme]README on GitHub[/url] (the same text is shown in the mod settings).

[h2]How it works[/h2][list]
[*]Turn each part on or off in [i]Options > Mod settings > Vanilla Tune-Up[/i] (restart to apply).
[*]Fixes are on by default. Tweaks marked (on by default) only change the interface or wait until you use them; other tweaks are off until you turn them on.
[*]If one part fails to load (for example after a game update), only that part is turned off.
[/list]

[h2]Fixes[/h2][list]
[*][b]Faction leaders:[/b] fixes [i]Faction leader for X is null[/i] by generating a new leader.
[*][b]History graph:[/b] removes the History tab lag in long-running colonies.
[*][b]Right-click menu:[/b] no more stutter while a right-click menu is open on the map.
[*][b]Trade setup:[/b] no freeze when a trade window opens with many items, Auto Seller included.
[*][b]Mechs in sieges:[/b] fixes [i]Exception while ticking lord with job RimWorld.LordJob_Siege[/i] with modded mechs.
[*][b]Prisoner food and tending:[/b] wardens respect food policy during mental breaks, tend bleeding prisoners before feeding, and feed a whole cell in one trip.
[*][b]Banished slaves free their bed[/b] (Ideology): instead of keeping it after they leave.
[*][b]Ejected genepacks stay out[/b] (Biotech): haulers no longer carry them straight back into the gene bank.
[*][b]Hungry pets find food across the map:[/b] no more pets starving far from the kibble in a large base.
[*][b]Farskip home unloads prisoners[/b] (Royalty): their caravan cargo goes to storage, like after a normal caravan arrival.
[*][b]Psychic ritual invoker keeps the offering[/b] (Anomaly): vomiting no longer drops the offering and fails the ritual; a canceled ritual no longer leaves anyone staring at the spot.
[*][b]Aligned corner icons:[/b] the bottom-right search and codex buttons no longer push other icons off the grid.
[*][b]Official translation fixes:[/b] quest text shown as [i]ERR:[/i] and untranslatable generated text (currently Traditional Chinese).
[/list]

[h2]Tweaks[/h2][list]
[*][b]Xenogerm queue[/b] (Biotech): a bill-style queue for the gene assembler.
[*][b]Mute labor and birth sounds[/b] (Biotech).
[*][b]Remove fog of war:[/b] except where revealing would trigger something.
[*][b]Faction icons in caravan lists[/b] (on by default).
[*][b]Caravan encounters stay on the current map[/b] (on by default).
[*][b]Pin resources[/b] (on by default): double-click a resource to keep it shown.
[*][b]Nutrient paste stock[/b] (on by default): "dispense until you have X" for paste dispensers.
[*][b]Mod update notes[/b] (on by default, Steam): the main menu lists recently updated mods with their change notes.
[*][b]Break up social fights:[/b] bystanders may step in, and a bad mood means more insults and fights.
[*][b]Short names on the map[/b] (on by default): long pawn names no longer cover neighbours.
[*][b]Ghouls can wear apparel[/b] (Anomaly): still no weapons.
[*][b]Caravan: abandon all dead at once[/b] (on by default).
[*][b]Taskbar letter badge[/b] (on by default, Windows): shows how many letters are waiting.
[*][b]Right-click to dismiss bundled letters[/b] (on by default).
[*][b]Visitors take shelter:[/b] caravans and visitors wait indoors from bad weather or danger instead of leaving early.
[*][b]Confirm before closing the game window[/b] (on by default).
[*][b]Slaves use prisoner beds[/b] (Ideology): no separate slave rooms or hospital needed.
[*][b]Prisoners help out by personality:[/b] kind ones tend and feed cellmates, hard-working ones clean.
[*][b]Calmer prison breaks and slave rebellions:[/b] mood, pain and traits decide who breaks out; recreation calms them; wardens arrest lone escapees.
[*][b]Hide slaves from the colonist bar[/b] (Ideology).
[*][b]Practice on trees for recreation:[/b] Shooting or Melee passion pawns train on trees, harmlessly.
[*][b]Background music button[/b] (on by default): mute the music or scroll to change its volume.
[*][b]Cycle keys for prisoners, visitors, animals and mechs:[/b] [b],[/b] and [b].[/b] cycle through pawns of the selected kind.
[*][b]Camera follows the pawn it jumped to[/b] (on by default): after [b],[/b] / [b].[/b], a portrait double-click, an alert or a letter.
[*][b]Psychic rituals sorted by psychic sensitivity[/b] (Anomaly, on by default).
[*][b]Smelt and destroy bills: keep X of each[/b] (on by default): the most valuable X of each item stay.
[*][b]Readers face the way their seat faces[/b] (on by default): no more reading backwards on a chair because a dresser or end table stands next to it.
[*][b]Camera jumps avoid the open inspect tab[/b] (on by default): the colonist is centred beside the tab, not under it.
[*][b]Field tending and cheaper medicine:[/b] bleeding is stopped where the patient lies; minor wounds get the cheapest medicine.
[*][b]Quicksave, save and quit, Continue:[/b] F5 quicksave plus Save and quit and Continue buttons.
[*][b]Screensaver[/b] (on by default): when idle, hides the interface and follows random colonists, cutting to events; letters and messages stay usable.
[*][b]Refugees change their clothes[/b] (Royalty): hospitality refugees follow an apparel policy.
[*][b]Default policies for new prisoners and slaves:[/b] the food policy you pick, plus an apparel policy for slaves.
[*][b]Visitors leave colony food alone:[/b] until their own runs out and they get hungry enough.
[*][b]Guards for dangerous hunts:[/b] armed colonists escort the hunter when the animal could fight back.
[*][b]Hybrid children keep a matching xenotype[/b] (Biotech).
[*][b]Tell faction colors apart:[/b] near-identical faction colors are nudged apart.
[*][b]Drag to reorder:[/b] right-drag the bottom bar, architect categories, buildings and command buttons.
[*][b]Compact colonist bar:[/b] shrinks while the mouse is away, expands on hover.
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2][list]
[*]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save. Removing it only drops xenogerm queues; with Break up social fights on, the first load logs one harmless error per leftover fight memory.
[*]Load after Harmony. English and Traditional Chinese included.
[/list]
