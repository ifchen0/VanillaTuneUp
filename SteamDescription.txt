Small fixes and optional tweaks for vanilla RimWorld, collected in one mod. Every part has its own toggle.

[h2]How it works[/h2][list]
[*]Each fix and tweak can be turned on or off in [i]Options > Mod settings > Vanilla Tune-Up[/i]. Changes take effect after restarting the game.
[*]Fixes are on by default. Tweaks change how the game plays or sounds, so they are off until you turn them on.
[*]If one part fails to load (for example after a game update), only that part is turned off and the rest keep working.
[/list]

[h2]Fixes[/h2][list]
[*][b]Faction leaders:[/b] fixes the [i]Faction leader for X is null[/i] error after loading a save. A new leader is generated the same way vanilla replaces a leader who died.
[*][b]History graph:[/b] removes the History tab lag in long-running colonies. Curves and event marks are drawn once into a texture and redrawn only when the data or the shown range changes. In a colony with 21,900 records per curve and 2,500 event marks, the tab went from 15-19 ms to under 1 ms per frame. The graph looks the same.
[*][b]Right-click menu:[/b] stops the stutter while a right-click menu is open on the map. Options are re-checked every 5 seconds instead of every 4th frame; choosing an option is still checked as in vanilla.
[*][b]Trade setup:[/b] removes the long freeze when a trade window opens in a colony with many items. With Auto Seller, its trades no longer rebuild the trade list a second time (11 seconds to 0.3 seconds per trade with about 3,000 item stacks).
[*][b]Mechs in sieges:[/b] fixes the error spam ([i]Exception while ticking lord with job RimWorld.LordJob_Siege[/i]) when a sieging group contains mechs added by other mods. Pawns without skills defend the camp instead of being picked as builders.
[*][b]Food policy during mental breaks:[/b] wardens no longer bring restricted food to a prisoner having a mental break. A pawn in a mental break that feeds itself behaves as in vanilla.
[*][b]Tend bleeding prisoners first:[/b] wardens skip feeding and food delivery while a prisoner is still bleeding, so they can move on to tending. Manual feeding orders still work.
[/list]

[h2]Tweaks[/h2][list]
[*][b]Xenogerm queue[/b] (Biotech): a bill-style queue for the gene assembler. Each entry can be done X times, until you have X, or forever. Skipping an entry keeps its progress.
[*][b]Mute labor and birth sounds[/b] (Biotech): mutes the labor (early/late) and birth sounds. Other baby sounds are unchanged.
[*][b]Remove fog of war:[/b] reveals the whole map when it is generated or loaded. A map stays fogged if revealing it would set something off (sleeping enemies such as ancient dangers, quest or reveal signals, entity codex or hidden item discoveries), so those maps play as in vanilla. Rock, ore and collapsed rocks stay hidden until they are reached by mining, and pocket maps such as the labyrinth are never revealed.
[*][b]Hybrid children keep a matching xenotype[/b] (Biotech): a child labeled Hybrid whose germline genes match one heritable xenotype by more than 80% (genes weighted by complexity, skin and hair color ignored) gets that xenotype instead (a tie is decided at random), so the name carries on to later generations. Checked at birth and on every load, so existing hybrids are covered too. Genes are never changed, and pawns with xenogenes are skipped. An option (off by default) also checks modified pawns: all genes, germline and xenogenes alike, are matched against every xenotype, for hybrids with xenogenes and Baseliners still shown with the default faceless icon (such as xenogerm implants); custom xenotypes given their own icon are left alone.
[*][b]Drag to reorder:[/b] hold the right mouse button and drag to reorder the bottom bar, the architect categories, the buildings inside them and the command buttons of whatever you select. Command buttons share one order across colonists, animals, mechs and buildings. A plain right click still works as before.
[*][b]Compact colonist bar (experimental):[/b] colonist bar entries shrink to two thirds of their height and the rows move closer together while the mouse is away, and expand again when the mouse moves over the bar; a row opens once the mouse rests on it for a moment. While collapsed, names and status icons stay visible and weapons are hidden; each can be changed in the settings. Optional taller portraits (on by default) show the whole pawn inside a taller entry, with adjustable height, zoom, offset and per-race zoom; weapons under portraits are kept clear of the next row. If [i][NL] Custom Portraits[/i] is active, it keeps control of the portrait size. Hover magnify (on by default): the portrait under the mouse grows a little wider and larger, and clicks in the gap next to a portrait go to that portrait.
[/list]
