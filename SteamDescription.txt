Small fixes and optional tweaks for vanilla RimWorld, collected in one mod.

[h2]How it works[/h2][list]
[*]Turn each part on or off in [i]Options > Mod settings > Vanilla Tune-Up[/i] (restart to apply).
[*]Fixes are on by default; tweaks are off until you turn them on.
[*]If one part fails to load (for example after a game update), only that part is turned off.
[/list]

[h2]Fixes[/h2][list]
[*][b]Faction leaders:[/b] fixes the [i]Faction leader for X is null[/i] error by generating a new leader, as vanilla does when one dies.
[*][b]History graph:[/b] removes the History tab lag in long-running colonies (15-19 ms to under 1 ms per frame in a large test colony).
[*][b]Right-click menu:[/b] stops the stutter while a right-click menu is open on the map.
[*][b]Trade setup:[/b] removes the freeze when a trade window opens in a colony with many items, including Auto Seller trades.
[*][b]Mechs in sieges:[/b] fixes the [i]Exception while ticking lord with job RimWorld.LordJob_Siege[/i] spam when a siege includes modded mechs.
[*][b]Food policy during mental breaks:[/b] wardens no longer bring restricted food to a prisoner having a mental break.
[*][b]Tend bleeding prisoners first:[/b] wardens skip feeding while a prisoner is bleeding, so tending comes first.
[*][b]Prisoner food delivery keeps up:[/b] when prisoners get hungry, one trip brings enough for everyone in the cell who needs food.
[*][b]Official translation fixes:[/b] fixes quest text shown as [i]ERR:[/i] and generated text left in English that a translation file cannot override (currently Traditional Chinese).
[/list]

[h2]Tweaks[/h2][list]
[*][b]Xenogerm queue[/b] (Biotech): a bill-style queue for the gene assembler (X times, until you have X, or forever).
[*][b]Mute labor and birth sounds[/b] (Biotech).
[*][b]Remove fog of war:[/b] reveals the map on generation or load, except fogged areas where revealing would trigger something. Rock and ore stay hidden until mined.
[*][b]Faction icons in caravan lists:[/b] shows the faction icon of guests, prisoners and other non-colony pawns.
[*][b]Caravan encounters stay on the current map:[/b] caravan event dialogs (meetings, demands, mod events such as Real Ruins) open without switching to the world map.
[*][b]Pin resources:[/b] left-click a resource in the categorized resource list to keep it shown while its category is collapsed, even at 0.
[*][b]Nutrient paste stock:[/b] a "dispense until you have X" target for nutrient paste dispensers, shared by the whole map.
[*][b]Mod update notes[/b] (Steam version): the main menu's top-left box (translation credits) lists your active Workshop mods, most recently updated first, with newly updated ones highlighted. Click one to read its Workshop change notes. Works for every mod, no support from the mod author needed.
[*][b]Break up social fights:[/b] anyone who sees a social fight nearby and is not hostile to the fighters may step in to stop it. Social skill helps; a failed attempt can turn it into a brawl with the mediator. Rivals and Bloodlust pawns may join in instead. Fighters remember who stepped in.
[*][b]Short names on the map:[/b] long names under pawns are cut to about one and a half cells wide, following the zoom, so they do not cover their neighbours. Hover over or select the pawn to see the full name.
[*][b]Food policy for new prisoners:[/b] new prisoners get the food policy you pick, and go back to the default when recruited.
[*][b]Hybrid children keep a matching xenotype[/b] (Biotech): a Hybrid child whose genes closely match one heritable xenotype gets that xenotype. Genes are never changed. Optional check for modified pawns.
[*][b]Tell faction colors apart:[/b] on load, factions with nearly identical colors are nudged apart (adjustable, saved with the game).
[*][b]Drag to reorder:[/b] right-drag to reorder the bottom bar, architect categories, buildings and command buttons.
[*][b]Compact colonist bar:[/b] the colonist bar shrinks while the mouse is away and expands on hover. Options for what stays visible, taller portraits and hover magnify.
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2][list]
[*]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save. Removing it only drops xenogerm queues; a xenogerm in progress continues as normal. If Break up social fights was on, the first load also logs one harmless error per leftover fight memory.
[*]Load after Harmony.
[*]English and Traditional Chinese translations included.
[/list]
